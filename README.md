# Everyworks Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Everyworks connects households and businesses in Singapore with professionals for home maintenance, cleaning, renovation, and interior design. The proposed homepage helps visitors find a service or start with Smart Diagnosis, where Everyworks assesses a WhatsApp enquiry and assigns a suitable service team.

## Main Improvements

1. **Organise the service catalogue.** Repeated listings make the original homepage crowded. Four dedicated sections for maintenance, cleaning, renovation, and interior design would feature the most requested services and link directly to their detail pages and enquiry options.
2. **Explain Smart Diagnosis and who handles the work.** Visitors need to know what happens after they send a message. A prominent walkthrough would show how Everyworks assesses requests and selects service partners, explain who handles quotations, scheduling, workmanship, and after-service support, and clarify the terms of the 30-day Everyworks Guarantee.
3. **Clarify prices, reviews, and enquiry options.** Different pricing models and repeated WhatsApp prompts make the next step unclear. Consistent summaries would distinguish fixed prices, estimates, and inspection-based quotations. Review figures would show their source, and service-specific WhatsApp links would prefill the enquiry. A persistent mobile WhatsApp button would keep contact within reach.

## Design Improvements

Distinct visual sections would make the four categories easy to scan. A step-by-step Smart Diagnosis walkthrough and consistent pricing layouts would help visitors understand the process and choose how to enquire.
